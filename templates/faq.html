Need To Know

COMMON QUESTIONS

Browse Men, Women or Cosmetics, select a product, choose the available size where applicable, add it to your cart and continue to checkout.

Tokyo Trends currently supports online payments through UPI, credit/debit card and net banking.

If your payment fails, check your payment details and try again. If the issue continues, contact Tokyo Trends support before placing another order.

Orders below ₹2,000 currently have a ₹99 shipping fee. Orders of ₹2,000 or more qualify for free shipping.

Use the Track My Order page and enter your Order ID and mobile number to check your order status.

Open the product detail page and check the available sizes and size chart before adding the product to your cart.

Size availability depends on the individual product and current stock. Available sizes are shown on the product detail page.

Discounted products show their original price, sale price and applicable discount on the shopping pages and product details.

The TOKYO100 coupon currently provides ₹1,000 off when the order subtotal is at least ₹8,000.

Tokyo Trends uses a simple mobile and email based customer account system. You can log in using your registered mobile number and email address.

Please keep the product and packaging and contact Tokyo Trends support as soon as possible with the relevant order information and photographs.

Use the contact information provided by Tokyo Trends on the website for assistance with your order or shopping questions.
